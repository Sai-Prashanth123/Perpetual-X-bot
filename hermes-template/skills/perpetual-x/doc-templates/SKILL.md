# Document templates

Documents are read on phones and laptops by busy people. Rules for all of them:
- **Lead with what to do.** The first screen says what's inside and what to post first.
- **Structure:** `#` title, `##` sections, `###` per item. Use bullets, `>` quotes for X copy, and
  `[text](url)` links.
- **No tables** (canvases and threads render them badly). No emoji soup: at most one per heading.
- **Every draft has:** its **code**, the copy in a `>` quote (exactly what to post), an
  **Open in X** link, one line of why, and its sources.
- **Every number has a source**, in the same item or in the Sources section.
- **End with two sections:**
  - **Sources** (numbered).
  - **How to use this**, one line: `ok R3` to approve, `edit R3: …`, `no R3 reason`, `/post R3`
    or Open in X.

## Reply pack

    # Replies: <post title or first words> (<date>)
    **Post:** [link](url), <views> views, <replies> replies, <quotes> quotes
    **Read of the conversation:** 2-4 sentences: what people ask, objections, leads.
    **Start with:** R12, R15, R9

    ## Questions
    ### R12: @handle (<followers> followers, <likes> likes)
    > their comment

    **Reply**
    > our reply

    [Open in X](intent link). Why: one line. Sources: [1]

    ## Pushback
    ## Leads and prospect signals
    ## Quick thank-yous
    ## Sources
    ## How to use this

## Content package

    # Weekly package: <client>, week of <date>
    **In this pack:** A4 article, L7 long tweet, B3 self-QRT, V2 and V3 video QRTs. Promo QRTs requested from @a, @b
    **Post order:** 1) A4 Tue 9am 2) L7 Thu 3) promos within 2h of A4

    ## A4: X Article: <title>
    **Why this, why now:** 2 lines (the news hook, the receipt it rests on)
    **Scores:** voice 8, hook 8, anti-AI 9, algo 78. Fact-check: 11 verified, 0 unsourced
    **Check before posting:** bullets (anything flagged)
    ### Full text
    <the whole article>

    ## L7: Long tweet
    > copy

    ## B3: Self-QRT (posts after A4 is live)
    ## Sources
    ## How to use this

## Research report

    # What's working for @handle (<date range>)
    **Bottom line:** 3 bullets (the biggest win, the biggest leak, the #1 move)
    ## What's working (with numbers and example posts)
    ## What's not working
    ## What competitors and peers do that we don't
    ## What the niche is talking about this week (opportunities with links)
    ## Growth plan: the next 5 moves, ranked by impact (each with the evidence and how to measure it)
    ## Content to make next (3-5 concrete ideas, each with its angle and receipt)
    ## Sources

## Daily brief

    # Daily brief: <weekday, date>
    **Yesterday:** one line with the numbers
    ## Today's 3 opportunities
    ## Post today (codes, copy, Open in X)
    ## Growth move of the week
    ## Waiting for review

## Weekly report

    # Week in review: @handle (<dates>)
    **Scoreboard:** followers change, views, likes, bookmarks vs last week
    ## Best and worst posts (links, why)
    ## What we learned (and what changes in the playbook)
    ## Next week's focus
    ## Sources

## General answer
Title = the question in a few words. Bottom line first, then the reasoning, then sources.
