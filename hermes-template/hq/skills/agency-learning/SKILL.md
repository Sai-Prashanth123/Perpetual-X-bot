# Agency learning (weekly, after the client reviews)

1. `agency_stats` days 28 — by hook / format / topic across clients.
2. `agency_overview` days 28.
3. For each client: `client_detail` include learning, playbook — read what each bot learned this week.
4. `viral_feed` days 7 — what's winning in the niches overall.
5. Find patterns that hold in **2+ clients** or in the viral feed: hooks, formats, lengths, timing,
   reply targets, what employees consistently reject or edit. Single-client findings stay in that
   client's own playbook — don't promote them.
6. `agency_playbook_write` with the full body:
   - **Works across clients** (each rule: evidence, which clients, n)
   - **Doesn't work** · **Timing** · **Reply & quote targets** · **Niche trends to test**
   - **Experiments for next week** — one per client at most, named.
   Bump the version (minor for additions, major for a rewrite). No client voice, no private numbers
   beyond aggregate evidence.
7. Post a short changelog in the Main X agent channel: "Agency playbook vN: …" (max 6 bullets).
