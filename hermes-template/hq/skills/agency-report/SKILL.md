# Agency report (weekly)

1. `agency_overview` days 7 and days 28. `agency_stats` days 7.
2. For the top and bottom client by views change: `client_detail` include series, stats, learning.
3. `viral_feed` days 7, limit 40. Optionally `x_user_tweets` for 2–3 watch accounts that keep
   showing up in the viral feed, to compare their output with our clients'.
4. Report (≤ 35 lines):
   1. **Headline** — agency-wide views, likes and followers vs last week.
   2. **Every client** — one line each: growth, best post, verdict (accelerating / steady / slipping).
   3. **What worked across clients** — hooks, formats, timing that won in more than one account (n).
   4. **Niche & competitors** — what's breaking out that our clients aren't doing yet.
   5. **Team** — consistency and follow-through per employee, with one coaching note each.
   6. **Next week** — 3 agency-level priorities.
